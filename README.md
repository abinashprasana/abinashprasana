## 👋 About Me

I am an MSc Artificial Intelligence student at Dublin Business School in Dublin, Ireland, graduating in September 2026. My work focuses on building end-to-end AI systems including RAG pipelines, deep learning models, NLP classifiers, and explainable ML tools, all built on real-world datasets.

Before my MSc I worked as an **Associate Analyst at Deloitte** in Hyderabad, supporting HIPAA-compliant healthcare IT systems including Epic EMR and ServiceNow in a regulated enterprise environment.

## 🔭 What I Am Currently Building

- RAG systems for clinical and research Q&A using FAISS, sentence transformers and local LLMs

- Explainable recommender systems using SHAP and LIME with popularity bias analysis
- End-to-end ML pipelines from raw data cleaning through to deployed Streamlit and Flask dashboards
